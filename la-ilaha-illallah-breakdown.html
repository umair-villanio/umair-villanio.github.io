لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ
The Declaration of Tawheed
لَا
LA
Absolutely, truly NO. Complete negation. None. Zero.
إِلَٰهَ
ILAHA
A god. Any deity. Anything worshipped. Any object of devotion.
إِلَّا
ILLA
Except. The turning point. After denying everything...
ٱللَّٰهُ
ALLAH
THE God. Al-Ilah. The One. The Only. The Definite. No other.

There is absolutely no god — no deity, no object of worship, no authority, no sustainer — except THE God, Allah alone.

☼

There is only one Sun. We don't say "a sun" — we say "THE Sun".
Similarly, there is no "a god" — there is only "THE God".

Allah = Al-Ilah = THE God.

Now replace Ilaha & Allah with the 99 Names
